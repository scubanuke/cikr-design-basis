What is a design basis?

Every facility on this site is built to do something. A water system delivers water, a power plant generates power, a 911 center answers calls. That job is its function. Safety is not the function; it is a requirement on how the function is done. Nothing is built simply to be safe. It is built to do something, and it must do that thing safely.

A design basis is the written promise of what the facility must withstand while it does its job, and what counts as success when it is tested. This work writes that promise in three layers, each building on the one before.

The design basis names the function, the people, process and technology that deliver it, and the limits within which it is done. Success is simple: the function is delivered within those limits.

The design basis accident adds the events experience has taught us to expect: fire, flood, storm, a failed component, an operator's error, a process flaw nobody anticipated. These are bounded because experience is bounded. Success means the facility either keeps working or reaches a safe, known condition, and then recovers with the help of the outside world: off-site power, repair crews, supplies, emergency services.

That second layer quietly assumes three things. Events are indifferent to how the facility responds. Each event runs its course and ends. And outside help remains available. Deliberate military action, the third layer, is defined by an adversary who removes those assumptions. It chooses the moment and adapts (intentionality). It keeps coming (persistence). It aims at the parts whose loss is hardest to recover from (precision). And it blocks repair and outside help (denial). An actor counts as this kind of adversary by what it can do, not by who it is.

So success changes. Recovery can no longer be counted on, because the adversary can defeat it. The facility must instead reach and hold a protected state, keeping the harm bounded, using only what is at the facility, for as long as the attack lasts. It may stop doing its job. It never stops doing it safely.

Two things move in opposite directions as you go from left to right in the picture. The conditions a design must withstand widen, and so the designs that meet them narrow. And everything rests on a single function held constant through all three layers, which is what makes them one progression rather than three separate analyses.

The real events on this site run the other way. A reference event reads something that actually happened back through the design basis and asks whether the method was enough. What it finds goes back to revise the design basis. Most of those events involved no adversary at all, and that is part of the lesson: a design that cannot survive the accident will not survive the attack.
