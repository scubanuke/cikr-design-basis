The Melamine Adulteration of Milk in China, 2008

Event date: 2008 · Read from instrument version 0.2 · Industry and Economy collection

What happened

In 2008 raw milk in China was diluted and laced with melamine at collection stations to pass tests that measured nitrogen as a stand-in for protein, and tainted products reached twenty-two makers of infant formula. About 294,000 children were diagnosed, more than 50,000 hospitalized, and six died. China then tested every batch of raw milk for melamine and prosecuted those responsible.

Tier

Adversarial, not military action. A deliberate crime for profit, not military action: attributed to no state.

Findings

  • F-1, Test the property, not the proxy. The tests measured nitrogen and could not tell melamine from protein.
  • F-2, Someone watches the dependent population's health. Months passed between the first kidney stones and the link to formula.
  • F-3, The stretch between farm and plant belongs to a design basis. No single agency held the collection stations where milk was adulterated.

For asset owners

Annex A of the instrument, in full. It is written for owners and operators, and it states no requirement.

A.1 What This Annex Is

This annex is for food processors and dairies that buy raw materials through collectors and intermediaries, especially for infant and other first-band products, and for the public health authorities that watch the health of dependent populations. It is not a finding and states no requirement. The recommendations of Section 8 are addressed to the framework; this annex is addressed to an operator. It has three parts, each drawn from a section of the instrument: a self-check that turns the event’s elements (Table 4-1) into questions an operator answers about the operator’s own system (A.2); the actions published, each stated as its publisher’s, with this instrument’s own marked and tied to a finding (A.3); and a plain-language account an operator can give a board (A.4).

A “no” or an “I don’t know” is not a failure of the operator; it is that operation’s finding, and the place to start.

A.3 What to Do Now

A.3.1 Test the Property, Not the Proxy

After the event, China stationed inspectors at the major dairy manufacturers, tested every batch of raw milk for melamine, and set interim limits for melamine in infant formula and other foods, and an expert meeting of the World Health Organization set a tolerable daily intake. These steps add the instrument’s own, from F-1: test what you receive for the property you need, not a proxy an adulterant can fool, and control the raw materials your suppliers sell you.

A.3.2 Hold the Stretch Between Farm and Plant, and Watch the Population

These steps are this instrument’s, from F-2 and F-3. Make someone responsible for the stretch between farm and plant; and make sure someone watches the health of the population that depends on your product, so that a rise in illness is linked to its cause quickly.

A.4 For the Board

In 2008, in China, raw milk was diluted with water at collection stations and melamine, an industrial chemical rich in nitrogen, was added to make it pass tests that measured nitrogen as a stand-in for protein; melamine-laced protein materials were also sold to formula makers. Tainted products were found at twenty-two makers of infant formula. Pediatricians noticed rising numbers of infants with kidney stones in the spring, and the link to formula took months to confirm. About 294,000 children were diagnosed, more than 50,000 were hospitalized, and six died. China then tested every batch of raw milk for melamine, set limits, examined more than 20 million children free of charge, and prosecuted those responsible. It was a crime for profit, attributed to no state. The lessons are that tests must measure the property needed and not a proxy that can be fooled, that someone must hold the stretch between farm and plant, and that someone must watch the health of those who depend on the product. Any processor that buys raw materials through intermediaries has the same exposure. The questions in this annex show management whether this company does, and most of the answers cost little to find.
